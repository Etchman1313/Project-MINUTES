# PROJECT MINUTES

## Product Vision

The Archive is an interactive historical record.

It is the first chapter of The Uncivil War.

The visitor should feel as though they have accidentally gained access to official government records documenting the final years of the Long Emergency.

The website should never feel promotional.

It should feel inevitable.

## Narrative Rule

The visitor earns information.

Nothing important is given away immediately.

Every scroll answers one question while raising another.

## Final Goal
