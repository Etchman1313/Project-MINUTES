# Production Philosophy

Development follows production milestones rather than traditional software milestones.

Each milestone should result in a complete, reviewable experience—not simply additional code.

The goal is to continually increase immersion while protecting the mystery of the novel.

# Long-Term Vision

Future enhancements may include:

- Additional archive rooms
- Reader-exclusive artifacts
- Companion material for future novels
- Seasonal updates
- Author commentary
- Behind-the-scenes production notes

These enhancements should never interfere with the primary experience.

---

# North Star

Every milestone should move the project closer to one goal:

Visitors should leave believing they have briefly entered the National Archives of a country that never existed.

If a milestone does not support that illusion, it should be reconsidered.
