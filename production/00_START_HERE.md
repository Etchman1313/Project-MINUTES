# Welcome

If you are reading this document, you are joining the development of **Project MINUTES**, the official interactive digital experience for the novel **The Uncivil War** by Timothy Eytcheson.

This is **not** a traditional author website.

It is **not** a marketing landing page.

It is **not** an online encyclopedia for the novel.

Project MINUTES is an immersive, cinematic archival experience whose sole purpose is to create irresistible curiosity about the novel without revealing its story.

The website exists to make visitors ask questions.

The novel exists to answer them.

---

# Project Mission

Create an experience that feels like a visitor has become the **first civilian granted access to newly declassified national records** following the end of a seven-year American civil war.

Visitors are not customers.

Visitors are participants.

They are entering an archive.

---

# Core Principle

The website never answers a question that the novel answers better.

Every interaction should increase curiosity.

Nothing should reduce the value of reading the book.

---

# Current Creative Direction

The visitor enters through an authentication sequence.

After authorization, they gain access to **Cabinet Zero**, a conservation room inside the National Archives Public Records Restoration & Access Unit.

The archive preserves objects from the conflict.

The archive does not explain them.

Artifacts are evidence.

Not exposition.

---

# Emotional Journey

1. Unease
2. Authority
3. Curiosity
4. Examination
5. Reflection
6. Responsibility

Visitors should leave feeling entrusted with history rather than marketed to.

---

# Visual Language

The visual identity combines:

- National Archives
- Museum conservation laboratories
- HBO prestige drama
- Christopher Nolan precision
- Documentary realism

Avoid:

- Modern dashboards
- Glassmorphism
- Marketing layouts
- Flashy animations
- Excessive UI chrome

Everything should feel permanent, tactile and restrained.
