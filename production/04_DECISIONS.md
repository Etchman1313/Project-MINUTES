# Decision 001

## Title

The Website Exists to Create Curiosity

### Decision

The website will never answer a question that the novel answers better.

### Reasoning

The digital experience exists to increase the reader's desire to experience the novel.

Every feature should reinforce the value of reading the book rather than replacing it.

### Consequences

Artifacts imply story.

Artifacts never explain story.

---

# Decision 002

## Title

The Visitor's Identity

### Decision

The visitor is the first civilian granted access to newly declassified records.

### Reasoning

This transforms the visitor from a passive audience into an active participant.

The authentication sequence now has narrative meaning instead of being a decorative loading animation.

### Consequences

Every interaction reinforces the idea that access has been granted rather than navigation being provided.

---

# Decision 003

## Title

The Archive Is Preserved

### Decision

The archive is an active National Archives conservation laboratory.

It is not a ruined government bunker.

### Reasoning

A preserved archive communicates respect for history.

A ruined archive communicates apocalypse.

The novel is about remembering.

Not surviving.

### Consequences

Lighting becomes controlled.

Objects become curated.

Atmosphere becomes quiet rather than chaotic.

---

# Decision 004

## Title

Cabinet Zero

### Decision

Navigation begins inside Cabinet Zero.

Visitors discover artifacts instead of navigating menus.

### Reasoning

People remember physical experiences.

They do not remember navigation bars.

### Consequences

The table becomes navigation.

Artifacts become interaction.

The interface disappears.

---

# Decision 005

## Title

Evidence Instead of Exposition

### Decision

Every artifact should imply a larger story without revealing it.

### Reasoning

Curiosity is generated through implication.

Spoilers destroy curiosity.

### Consequences

No timelines.

No maps explaining events.

No plot summaries.

Only evidence.

---

# Decision 006

## Title

The Website Is an Experience

### Decision

The project is not an author website.

It is an interactive companion experience.

### Reasoning

The experience should stand on its own while naturally leading visitors toward the novel.

### Consequences

Every design decision is judged by immersion rather than marketing effectiveness.

---

# Decision 007

## Title

Vision Before Code

### Decision

The Vision Book becomes the visual source of truth.

Implementation follows approved production frames.

### Reasoning

Engineering should reproduce an approved creative vision rather than invent one.

### Consequences

Future implementation work begins with visual approval.

---

# Decision 008

## Title

One Hero Per Screen

### Decision

Each screen contains one dominant visual element.

### Reasoning

Strong cinematic composition relies on clear visual hierarchy.

### Consequences

Additional elements always support the hero rather than compete with it.

---

# Decision 009

## Title

Silence Is Content

### Decision

Negative space, restrained motion, and pauses are treated as storytelling devices.

### Reasoning

Not every moment requires activity.

Silence creates anticipation.

### Consequences

Animations become slower.

Pages become less cluttered.

Attention becomes intentional.

---

# Decision 010

## Title

The Production Bible

### Decision

The project will preserve both implementation and creative reasoning.

### Reasoning

Future development should never require rediscovering previous decisions.

### Consequences

Production documents become first-class project assets.
