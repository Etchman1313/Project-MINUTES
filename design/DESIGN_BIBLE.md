# Mission

The Archive is not a website.

It is an historical artifact from an America that almost existed.

Visitors should never feel like they are browsing a promotional page. They should feel like they have been granted access to an official archive documenting the final years of the Long Emergency.

The experience is cinematic rather than commercial.

The experience reveals rather than advertises.

Every interaction must deepen immersion.

# Design Philosophy

Christopher Nolan over Marvel.

HBO over Hollywood.

Precision over spectacle.

Silence over noise.

Objects over exposition.

The visitor discovers meaning.

Nothing is explained twice.
